How the gates work

A = 1 sends a glider; B = 1 sends a lightweight spaceship. Either alone knocks out an EQ clock pulse. With both present, the inputs collide with each other and the pulse gets through. With neither present, it passes freely.

At NOT, any surviving EQ pulse collides with a second clock pulse. If EQ was empty, that clock pulse continues to the blue NOT port.

Starting configuration

At generation 0, click or drag to paint or erase. Focus the grid and use arrows and Space to edit by keyboard. Shift-drag or use Pan mode to move the viewport.

How life works

A live cell survives with 2 or 3 neighbors. An empty cell comes alive with exactly 3. All other cells die or stay empty.

Life continues outside the viewport. Playback stops when no living cells remain anywhere in the world.
