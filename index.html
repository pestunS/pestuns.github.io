Starting configuration

At generation 0, click or drag to paint or erase. Keyboard: focus the grid, use arrow keys to move, and Space to toggle a cell. Return to the starting grid to edit again.

How life works

A live cell survives with 2 or 3 neighbors. An empty cell comes alive with exactly 3. All other cells die or stay empty.

Edges are fixed; cells outside the grid are empty. Playback stops when no living cells remain.
